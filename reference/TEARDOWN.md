## Arc

It's a 20-second kinetic poem about personal change. It asks a question, refuses to answer it in words, then shows the answer as a person's "inventory" of interests (pixel-art objects) and three virtues. It lands on the word LOVE. Structure: **question, rebuttal, show (inventory), three values, scatter/dissolve, thesis, coda.**

| Act | Time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Question | 0.00 to 3.80 | Handwritten and typed text builds "how do you communicate that you're going through a change?" |
| 2. Rebuttal | 3.80 to 6.20 | Thermal-silhouette profile head: "you dont." then "you just show it." |
| 3. Show | 6.20 to 8.30 | A ring of 12 pixel-art objects (the person's interests) rotates, with items flashing one by one |
| 4. Three values | 8.30 to 11.90 | Dark word cards alternate with light object strips: action. / intention. / curiosity. |
| 5. Scatter | 11.90 to 13.80 | The ring reforms, explodes outward, and spray-paint blobs blacken the objects |
| 6. Thesis | 13.80 to 17.20 | Thermal-silhouette hand: "through ones own ability to", then L O V E spelled one letter per cut, each paired with an object |
| 7. Coda | 17.20 to 20.46 | L O V E letters drift on paper with red pen scribble loops, then a final push-in |

## Motion and transition vocabulary

- **Typewriter with block cursor:** each word card opens with a solid white rectangle where the word will be, which resolves into the word with a thin blinking caret to its right. About 0.15 s per reveal. *(measured from strips)*
- **Blob-to-thermal resolve:** a heavily blurred purple/magenta/red shape snaps into focus as the orange-yellow heat-map silhouette over about 0.3 s. Used for both human shots. *(observed; the curve is inferred as ease-out)*
- **Redaction bar:** a solid black bar covers words and slides across to reveal new ones. Used at 0.30 s and 13.6 s.
- **Design-tool overlays:** dashed guide lines, dotted bounding box with handles. These frame the text as "being edited", which fits the change theme.
- **Ink/spray-paint:** black airbrush blobs land on sprites and blacken them, plus fast calligraphic brush swooshes as transition wipes (13.7, 17.25).
- **Gold flash pop:** a sprite gets a yellow starburst and soft orange bloom for about 0.15 s, as if "selected".
- **3D sprite tumble:** pixel sprites are not flat. They have depth and rotate in 3D (camera flips edge-on at 8.95 s).
- **Background color ramps at scene ends:** black to mauve to off-white (5.7 to 6.15, 15.6 to 15.75) right before a cut to a paper scene, so the cut feels motivated.
- **Light/dark alternation:** from 8.3 to 12 s every cut flips between black and paper, which creates a strobing rhythm.
- **Red pen loops:** thin red scribble ellipses draw around letters in the coda, like a teacher's annotations.
- **Final push-in:** about a 1.5x scale in the last 0.2 s. *(measured from the 20.10 vs 20.25 frames)*

## Design system

- **Palette** (manifest): paper `#d9d9da`/`#d6d6d6`, near-black `#151515`/`#171617`, signal red `#cd1f15`/`#c5031b`, deep red `#a50e1d`, thermal orange `#d3631a`, thermal yellow `#d89624`, coin gold `#dab418`, cash green `#67982c`, ink brown `#715d57`. Cyan/blue are used only as sparing accents (S01 flare, LOVE letter flashes).
- **Type:** one lowercase rounded geometric sans (Product Sans / Gilroy-like, *inferred*), white on dark and brown-black on paper. Words end with a period. Captions are small (about 0.035 to 0.05 H), so the imagery dominates. The one exception is the oversized "how" in the opening. Handwriting is a separate monoline pen layer.
- **Imagery:** 12 pixel-art sprites with chunky dark outlines, rendered with 3D extrusion. Two live-action silhouettes with a heat-map gradient map.
- **Texture:** heavy film grain on everything, paper fiber on light scenes, soft vignettes, slight chromatic/glow bloom on whites. 4:3 frame for a film/scan feel.
- **Layout:** almost everything sits on the horizontal center line (y about 0.5). Object left, word right on word cards. Figure center, caption split on both sides.

## Sound

- Music-driven. Onsets are steady at about 0.25 to 0.3 s apart (8th notes at roughly 100 to 120 BPM, *inferred*).
- Every cut lands within 0.15 s of an onset, and 9 of 14 land within 0.05 s, so the edit is **beat-locked**.
- Loudness: quiet dip from 2.3 to 4.0 s (under the question and the head reveal), a peak at 6.5 s (ring reveal), dense hits from 8 to 12 s (word cards), peaks again from 15.5 to 17.2 (LOVE spell-out), then it drops to near silence for the coda.
- **Not done:** no speech transcription. There's no local Whisper on this machine. The on-screen text reads like a spoken poem, so there may be a voiceover. Check by ear.

## Why it works

1. **It answers its own question with form.** The question "how do you communicate change?" is answered "you dont. you just show it." Then the video literally stops talking and shows a montage of objects. The structure is the argument.
2. **Two visual worlds that rhyme.** Warm thermal humans on black vs. cold pixel objects on paper. Alternating them, and accelerating that alternation in the middle, creates rhythm without needing camera moves.
3. **Small type, big image.** Captions stay tiny and centered, so each cut reads in under a second.
4. **Pacing contrast.** Slow open (about 2 s beats), a fast middle (0.5 to 0.9 s beats, then 0.15 to 0.3 s for LOVE), and a long, nearly silent 3.3 s coda. Speed is used for emphasis, not as a constant.
5. **Hand-made texture over digital assets.** Grain, paper, ink blobs, pen loops and redaction bars make clean pixel art and rotoscoped footage feel crafted and analog.
6. **The thesis word is earned.** LOVE is spelled one letter per beat, each paired with an object from the inventory, so the word is literally made of the person's interests.
